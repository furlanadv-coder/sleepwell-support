Frequently Asked Questions

Does SleepWell Sounds require an internet connection?

No. The included sounds are available offline.

Can sounds continue when the iPhone is locked?

Yes. SleepWell Sounds supports background audio playback.

How many sounds can I mix?

You can play up to three sounds simultaneously and adjust the volume of each sound individually.

Does SleepWell Sounds include a sleep timer?

Yes. You can select 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4, 6 or 8 hours, or use continuous playback.

Does SleepWell Sounds require an account?

No. No account, registration or login is required.

About SleepWell Sounds

SleepWell Sounds provides relaxing offline sounds including rain, water, nature, fire, ambient sounds and noise options. Create your own relaxing environment by mixing up to three sounds and adjusting their individual volume levels.
