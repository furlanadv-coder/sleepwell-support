Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SleepWell Sounds handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

SleepWell Sounds does not require account creation, registration or login.

SleepWell Sounds does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information through the application.

Offline Functionality

The sounds included with SleepWell Sounds are designed to be available offline. Playback, sound mixing, individual volume adjustments and sleep timer functionality are performed locally on your device.

Analytics and Advertising

SleepWell Sounds does not use third-party advertising services and does not use third-party analytics services to track users across apps or websites.

Accounts

SleepWell Sounds does not require or provide user accounts. No registration or login is necessary to use the application.

Data Sharing

Because SleepWell Sounds does not collect personal information through the application, we do not sell or share personal information collected through the app with third parties.

Children's Privacy

SleepWell Sounds does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements. The current version will be made available on this page.
